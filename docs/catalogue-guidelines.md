# Catalogue guidelines

Read this guide before changing catalogue landmarks, probabilities, source-backed copy, artwork, relationships, translations, or scientific models.

## General catalogue rules

- Do not fabricate opposite events. Populate the likely side with independently meaningful events, including common compositions and naturally likely outcomes. A sourced failure event is valid when its setup and outcome are independently meaningful, such as one lottery line winning no main-draw prize.
- Prefer relatable, unitary events. State model assumptions, link sources, and date estimates whose inputs change.
- Describe each event first in one to three concise, approachable sentences for a general reader. Open with a concrete situation, consequence, or surprising scale when the event supports one. Keep formulas, long denominators, counting methods, and model qualifications in the calculation and assumptions fields. Avoid instructional templates, compressed fragments, patronizing definitions, invented comparisons, and forced questions.
- Explain chances through clear odds or frequencies that name the sampled unit and timeframe. Use average language for estimates and conditional, unit-appropriate counts in English and French. Do not make bare assertions that imply an event is inevitable.

## Observed estimates and teaching models

- Present dated reliability figures as observed historical aggregates with their reported unit, such as a customer-minute, service-use moment, delivery deadline, or commercial flight. Keep provider availability separate from account access, transaction approval, and guarantees for an individual customer.
- For roads, distinguish police-reported injury-collision involvement per vehicle distance from an annual breakdown survey. State any constant-distance Poisson assumption, retain the trip length, and do not recast vehicle involvement as occupant injury or death risk.
- Present trading-card pull rates as dated observed per-booster estimates for a named expansion. Preserve confidence intervals and exact rarity definitions. Do not generalize a sampled print run to every language or market.
- Model shared birthdays with independent dates uniformly distributed over 365 days, excluding 29 February. Show the regular 10-to-60-person sequence by computing the all-different tail directly and taking its complement.
- Derive diagnostic teaching examples from shared named inputs, with hypothetical prevalence and conditioning assumptions, and separate them from personal diagnosis or current measured prevalence. Prior and result states use the same hypothesis and population; name hypothetical prevalence separately from historical sensitivity and specificity.
- Derive expected true-positive, false-positive, false-negative, and true-negative counts from the same diagnostic inputs. Present likelihood ratios as odds multipliers and make each result card's opening explanation specific to that state.
- Keep DNA events explicit about sampled unit and timescale. Distinguish replication error per copied nucleotide from de novo variant per transmitted position per generation, and do not represent either as disease probability.

## Security, physical models, and astronomy

- Present common-password guess rates as dated observations from a named corpus with its processed-password denominator. Compare retained RockYou literal guesses with the same pinned SecLists frequency archive and its 32,603,388 total occurrences. Do not generalize either rate or dBan difference to current account-compromise risk.
- Generated-password probabilities state their alphabet, length, independence, and uniformity model. Prefer passkeys where available; otherwise advise unique manager-generated passwords and two-step verification without giving passkeys a synthetic probability.
- Model a six-word EFF passphrase as six independent uniform choices from the 7,776-word list, with order and repeats allowed. Keep AES landmarks conditional on one random key guess; practical security also depends on software and key handling.
- Model one EFF word as one uniform choice made by five ordered fair d6 results. Relate it to the six-word passphrase and dice examples; do not relate it to a matching-dice game.
- Keep the needle-in-a-hay-bale example conditional on uniform point sampling and stated cylindrical needle and bale dimensions. Do not present its volume ratio as the chance of finding a needle by hand.
- Model pool drops as uniform selection of one specified equal-volume portion. State faucet-drop volume and pool-water dimensions; make garden dimensions explicit and use the named competition pool for sourced dimensions.
- State uniform phase and a fixed synodic month for astronomy period examples. Global eclipse-year examples sample distinct calendar years from the stated NASA catalogue and distinguish total, partial, hybrid, and penumbral types. The local total-eclipse example is a long-run return-interval estimate for one representative fixed place, without weather or a named-location forecast.
- State the sampling measure for composition examples. Stellar composition examples sample equal-mass portions from an approximate representative 70% hydrogen, 28% helium, and 2% heavier-element mixture; do not present those shares as atom counts, exact values for every star, or an empirical galactic aggregate. Sample Solar System matter in proportion to mass for the Sun, and sample freshwater by water-equivalent volume.
- Keep astronomy population estimates tied to their denominators. The observed Earth-sized exoplanet share covers confirmed planets with a known radius in a dated archive snapshot and inherits detection bias. The main-sequence share samples stars by count. The stellar-black-hole share samples ordinary baryonic mass and serves as a rounded order-of-magnitude model.
- Present the massive-moon result as a simulation estimate. Distinguish its sensitivity range from a confidence interval or measured galactic frequency, and retain the host-planet and moon-mass thresholds.

## Games, weather, sport, and Enigma

- French Scrabble blanks are neither vowels nor consonants. Count Y among the 45 vowels and model a seven-tile rack as sampling without replacement.
- Treat all-dice-identical and at-least-one-pair outcomes as distinct families. For the latter, count every outcome with a repeated face, including larger matching groups. Keep a first-roll five-dice all-identical outcome distinct from an at-least-one-pair outcome. Draw any-match dice with fours and six-specific outcomes with sixes.
- Limit the Hold'em connector to the ten best-five-card outcomes from the shared seven-card model. Exclude standard-card, five-card draw, and blackjack examples. Limit the Destined Rivals connector to the three rarities from that booster model.
- Treat d20 critical outcomes as the cited attack-roll convention: natural 20 is a critical hit and natural 1 an automatic miss. Avoiding natural 1 does not guarantee success; keep modifiers, rerolls, advantage, and other systems outside the fair single-roll model.
- Label weather normals and completed-season sport rates as dated historical estimates. Define whether a sample is a calendar day, daylight moment, shot, serve, or penalty, and do not present an aggregate as a personal forecast.
- Keep Enigma configuration landmarks conditional on stated known settings, mechanically permitted rotor choices, and exactly 10 plugboard cables joining 20 letters. Treat them as uniform configuration-guess spaces rather than effective cryptographic strength. Ordinary reciprocal family differences compare whole-configuration endpoints.
- Interpret sourced candidate reduction as evidence movement only when every other model factor stays fixed. Keep alignment confidence and Bombe work in historical context without inventing a whole-machine probability gain. Do not add separate gain controls to the radial scene.

## Artwork, links, and shared data

- Draw lottery landmarks as tickets with a country or European flag: “Jackpot!” for jackpot tickets, thumbs-up for any-prize events, and thumbs-down for no-prize events.
- Keep shared probabilities, positions, artwork structure, relations, and navigation behavior common across locales. Localize copy, accessibility labels, locale-sensitive numbers, and text embedded in artwork.
- Every relationship and advice link is reciprocal and targets an existing landmark. Preserve stable display order and full-catalogue angles when filtering regional references.
